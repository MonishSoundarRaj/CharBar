# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: August 01, 2026_

This Privacy Notice for CharBar ("we," "us," or "our") describes how and why we might access, collect, store, use, and/or share ("process") your personal information when you use our services ("Services"), including when you:

- Use CharBar. CharBar is a macOS menu bar utility that packs multiple productivity tools (Pomodoro timer, meeting countdown, now-playing music player, Bluetooth monitoring, and system stats) into animated Lottie character icons in the menu bar. It can run as traditional menu bar icons or switch to a floating bar mode that replaces the Dock when an external display is connected.
- Engage with us in other related ways, including any marketing or events.

**Questions or concerns?** Reading this Privacy Notice will help you understand your privacy rights and choices. If you do not agree with our policies and practices, please do not use our Services. If you still have questions or concerns, please contact us by opening an issue at https://github.com/MonishSoundarRaj/CharBar/issues.

**Summary:** The CharBar app runs entirely on your Mac. System stats, media information, Bluetooth state, and calendar data are read on-device to power the interface and are never transmitted to any server. The sections below describe the full policy, including any information handled by our download website.

---

## 1. What information do we collect?

### Personal information you disclose to us

_In short: We collect personal information that you provide to us._

We collect personal information that you voluntarily provide to us when you express an interest in obtaining information about us or our products and Services, when you participate in activities on the Services, or otherwise when you contact us. The personal information we collect may include names and email addresses (for example, if you request a download or contact us). We do not process sensitive information.

**Application data.** The CharBar app may request access or permission to certain features of your Mac, including Bluetooth, calendar, and accessibility (on-device only): CPU, GPU, RAM, battery, network, storage usage, media playback status, and global keyboard inputs (via accessibility permissions). All of this data is processed locally on your Mac to enable app functionality and is never transmitted to our servers. You can change these permissions at any time in System Settings.

### Information automatically collected

_In short: Some information, such as your IP address and/or browser and device characteristics, may be collected automatically when you visit our website._

When you visit our download website, we may automatically collect standard technical information such as IP address, browser and device characteristics, operating system, language preferences, and referring URLs. This information is primarily needed to maintain the security and operation of the website and for internal analytics. The CharBar app itself does not collect this information.
